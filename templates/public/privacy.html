Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to DevWorkspace. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any student and workspace data processed through our platform. This policy outlines our collection, storage, and handling practices.

2. Information Collection

We collect information provided directly by users when registering an account, including full names, usernames, and email addresses. We also track learning progress such as completed lessons, exercise submissions, and quiz metrics.

3. Workspace & File Data

Code files, documents, and project blueprints created or uploaded in the Code Workspace remain the intellectual property of the student. In this frontend stage, files are managed locally within browser storage.

4. Local Storage Usage

DevWorkspace utilizes browser localStorage to store interface preferences, dark/light theme choices, accent color selections, and frontend session tokens. No third-party tracking cookies are deployed.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Users have full control over their saved state. Clearing browser application cache or using the "Reset Dashboard" action in Settings instantly purges demo workspace records.

6. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding this policy or data safety, reach out to our team at privacy@devworkspace.io.
